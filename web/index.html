⚠ Honest Limitations
Required Reading
  • Modest accuracy: The best model (LogisticRegression) achieves Macro-F1 ≈ 0.52 and AUROC ≈ 0.71. It is meaningfully better than random chance (0.22 / 0.50) but far from perfect.
  • GNN did NOT beat baselines: HeteroGraphSAGE (Macro-F1 ≈ 0.32) is substantially worse than logistic regression. This is a legitimate negative research finding — the available graph signal was insufficient. Reported as-is.
  • OA is association, not causation: The open-access citation advantage is an observational correlation. Author self-selection bias (high-impact authors choose OA) is not controlled. We do not claim OA causes more citations.
  • Venue OA/APC as-of-today: Venue rankings and APC data reflect current records; historical values may differ.
  • CD Disruption Index: Requires ≥5 of the paper's references to appear in our 16,500-paper corpus. Most papers will show null due to sparse corpus coverage.
