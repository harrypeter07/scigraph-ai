Strict Scientometric Prediction Workflow

SciGraph AI 2.0 adheres strictly to empirical citation dynamics and avoids future data leakage.

STEP 01
Ingest Metadata
Resolves OpenAlex entities, author collaborations, verified publication venues, and foundational references.
STEP 02
Temporal Freeze
All citation graphs and venue indicators are frozen at $T_{\text{cutoff}} = \text{pub\_year} + 2$. Zero future data is visible.
STEP 03
2-Hop GNN Encoding
Multi-relational HeteroSAGE aggregates author prestige, institutional ranking, and precursor knowledge domains.
STEP 04
Calibrated Forecast
Primary calibrated classifier generates probabilistic predictions against field-specific cohort percentiles.
