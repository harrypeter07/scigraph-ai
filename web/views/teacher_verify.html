Held-out
For experts

Cutoff: The moment 2 years after publication where feature observation stops. Any citation after this date is locked out.

Tier: Citation impact category relative to all papers in the same field and year: Low (<50th percentile), Medium (50th–90th percentile), or High (≥90th percentile).

Cohort: Empirical distribution of papers sharing the same publication year and discipline, used to set objective percentiles.

Calibration: Temperature scaling applied to model outputs so that predicted probabilities reflect true empirical error frequencies.
