PROBLEM STATEMENT
What problem are we addressing? Predicting whether a new paper will reach Low, Medium or High citation impact in the label window (years 3 to 5 after publication), using only what is known 2 years after publication.
Who is affected? Researchers, reviewers, funding bodies and universities that must judge new work before citations accumulate.
Current challenges: Tools only count citations after they happen; leakage is a known problem in citation forecasting; raw counts are unfair across fields.
Why is it important? Early, fair and leakage-audited forecasts support better evaluation, funding and discovery of influential research.

PROPOSED SOLUTION
1
Project idea
Forecast a paper’s citation impact class (Low / Medium / High) in the label window, years 3 to 5 after publication, using only data available 2 years after publication.
2
Proposed approach
Ingest {{n_works}} OpenAlex works, build a heterogeneous graph, train a 2-hop PyG HeteroGraphSAGE against five baselines (majority, cohort-rank, LR, LightGBM, MLP).
3
Key innovation
T_cutoff protocol with automated leakage tests, venue ranks (SCImago, CORE), DOAJ fees and calibrated probabilities in one graph model.
4
Expected outcome
A reproducible, explainable system (API + web UI), compared fairly against tabular baselines with confidence intervals. We report the result either way.
