Strict Scientometric Prediction Workflow

SciGraph AI 2.0 adheres strictly to empirical citation dynamics and avoids future data leakage.

All citation graphs and venue indicators are frozen at t ≤ t_pub + 2y. Zero future data is visible.

2-Hop GNN Encoding

STEP 04
Calibrated Forecast
Primary calibrated classifier generates probabilistic predictions against field-specific cohort percentiles.
